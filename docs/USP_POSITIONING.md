## Why Buyers Choose This Over Alternatives

### 1. Sovereignty (anti-SaaS positioning)
Customers who have been burned by Shopify, Wix, or ClickFunnels fee hikes respond to "you own the infrastructure." This is the primary emotional trigger for the target persona (digital entrepreneurs, 1–10 person companies).

### 2. AI-first, not AI-bolted-on
The swarm is the product. Competitors offer AI as a feature. We offer AI as the operating model — Scout finds leads, Ghostwriter crafts emails, Closer monitors replies, SEO Factory publishes content. No human required to keep the engine running.

### 3. Offer ladder is pre-designed
The $29/mo → $7,500 funnel is documented, implemented in the catalog API, and wired to Stripe. Competitors sell you tools; we sell you a business with revenue architecture already proven.

### 4. Windows Server 2025 / IIS compatibility
Most "AI stacks" assume Linux/Docker. The IIS-native deployment is a genuine niche differentiator for operators already on Windows hosting, corporate environments, or Azure IaaS.

## Non-Negotiable Differentiators to Protect

These must remain true to maintain the USP:

- Operator keeps full infrastructure ownership (no forced cloud vendor)
- Outreach swarm must be functional at launch, not "coming soon"
- Stripe offer ladder must be live with real products/prices at launch
- CI must be green on push to main — credibility signal for technical buyers
