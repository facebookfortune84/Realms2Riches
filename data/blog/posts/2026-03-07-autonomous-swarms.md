### What are Autonomous Swarms?

Autonomous swarms are systems composed of multiple agents that interact with each other and their environment to achieve a collective objective. These agents can be simple robots, drones, or even software entities that communicate and coordinate with each other to adapt to changing conditions. Autonomous swarms are characterized by their ability to:

* **Self-organize**: Agents adapt to their environment and adjust their behavior to achieve the collective goal.
* **Decentralize**: Decision-making is distributed among agents, eliminating the need for a centralized controller.
* **Adapt**: Agents learn from their experiences and adjust their behavior to optimize performance.
