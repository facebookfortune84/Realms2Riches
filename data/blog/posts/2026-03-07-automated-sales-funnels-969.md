**What are Automated Sales Funnels?**

Automated sales funnels are pre-designed, step-by-step processes that guide potential customers through a series of interactions, ultimately leading to a sale or conversion. These funnels are typically built using marketing automation software, which enables businesses to create, test, and optimize their sales funnels in real-time. By automating the sales process, businesses can:

* Increase conversion rates and revenue
* Reduce manual labor and improve efficiency
* Enhance customer experience and engagement
* Gain valuable insights into customer behavior and preferences
