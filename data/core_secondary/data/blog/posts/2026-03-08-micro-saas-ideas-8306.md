**What is Micro-SaaS?**
--------------------

Micro-SaaS is a type of software as a service (SaaS) that is designed to be small, focused, and easy to use. Unlike traditional SaaS, micro-SaaS solutions are typically built around a specific problem or pain point, and are designed to be simple and intuitive to use. This approach allows micro-SaaS businesses to be more agile and adaptable, and to focus on delivering value to their customers.

**Benefits of Micro-SaaS**
----------------------

So why choose micro-SaaS over traditional SaaS? Here are just a few benefits:

* **Lower overhead costs**: Micro-SaaS solutions require less infrastructure and maintenance than traditional SaaS, making them more cost-effective.
* **Faster development time**: Micro-SaaS solutions are typically smaller and more focused, allowing developers to build and launch them quickly.
* **Greater flexibility**: Micro-SaaS solutions can be easily adapted and updated to meet changing customer needs.
* **Improved customer satisfaction**: Micro-SaaS solutions are designed to be simple and intuitive to use, making it easier for customers to get the help they need.
